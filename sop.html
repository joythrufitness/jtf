Website Editor — How-To Guide

A step-by-step guide for updating the JTF website

This guide walks you through how to edit website content and publish changes live using Pages CMS. There's nothing to download and nothing to upload — you edit a field, click Save, and the site updates on its own within about a minute.

Part 1 — One-Time Setup
1

Open Pages CMS

Go to app.pagescms.org and click Sign in with GitHub. Use your GitHub account (the one with access to the JTF website repository).

2

Select the Website Repository

After signing in, choose joythrufitness / jtf from the list of repositories. Bookmark this page for next time — you'll land straight on the same editor.

Part 2 — Make Your Changes
3

Pick a Section

You'll see a list of sections — Hero, Mission, Personal Training, Time 4 U, Group Classes, Payment Info, Pricing, About, Trainers, Testimonials, Contact, Resources & Downloads, and Brand Assets. Click one to open just that part of the site — you don't need to scroll past the others.

4

Edit Text Fields

Click into any text box and type your changes. All fields work the same way — just click and edit.

5

Update an Image

When a field already has a photo, click the small trash icon on its corner first to clear it. Two buttons then appear in its place — Upload (pick a new photo from your computer) and Select (choose one already on the site). Click Upload and choose your file.

  • The other small icon (↗) just opens the current photo on GitHub to view it — it doesn't help with replacing it
  • Removing a photo doesn't take it off the live site until you click Save — if you clear one by mistake, just leave the page without saving instead of uploading a replacement
6

Add or Remove a Trainer / Testimonial / Class

These are lists. Use Add item at the bottom of the list to add a new entry, or the trash icon on an item to remove it.

7

Add or Remove a Downloadable Document

Open the Resources & Downloads section. Click Add item, fill in the Title, then click the PDF file field and upload the PDF. To remove one, use the trash icon on that entry.

💾
Nothing is saved until you click Save. Pages CMS doesn't auto-save. If you close the tab partway through, unsaved changes are lost — so save often, especially after a big edit.
Part 3 — Publish
8

Click "Save"

When you're done editing, click Save at the top of the page. That's it — no download, no GitHub upload screen.

9

Wait ~60 Seconds

The live website at joythrufitness.com updates automatically. Wait about a minute, then refresh the page to see your changes.

💡 If you don't see your changes, try a hard refresh: Cmd + Shift + R on Mac, or Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows.
